Bakery & café · Pugwash, NS

Water Street’s bakery — and yes, the donairs are famous.

Sheryl’s is Pugwash’s harbour-side bakery café: sourdough out of the oven, cinnamon rolls and tea biscuits for the coffee crowd, homemade preserves — and donairs people drive across the county for.

Rated 4.9 ★ on Google · 132 Water St, Pugwash, NS · Weekday mornings from 7 AM

Fresh Baking

Sourdough loaves, sweets and the day’s baking — the counter changes with the oven, so come early for the full pick.

Cinnamon Rolls & Tea Biscuits

The coffee-break classics, baked in-house — the reason half of Pugwash seems to be here by eight in the morning.

The Famous Donairs

Ask anyone in Cumberland County: Sheryl’s donairs have a reputation that travels well beyond Water Street.

Preserves & Pantry

Homemade preserves and pantry goods to take home — a taste of the bakery long after the bag is empty.
